# Skill: Hackathon Pitch & Demo Pack (`pitch-pack`)

A winning hackathon project is 50% code and 50% presentation. This skill prepares everything needed to impress judges in a 2-3 minute presentation.

## Pitch Pack Components:

### 1. The 2-Minute Demo Script (Structure)
- **0:00 - 0:20 (The Pain)**: Concrete problem statement and who suffers from it today.
- **0:20 - 0:40 (The Solution)**: 1-sentence value proposition and core breakthrough.
- **0:40 - 1:30 (The Live Demo)**: Show, don't tell. Walkthrough the single Hero Flow.
- **1:30 - 1:50 (Architecture & Innovation)**: Why this wasn't possible before (multi-model, agentic harness).
- **1:50 - 2:00 (The Vision & Ask)**: Next steps and market impact.

### 2. Pitch Deck Outline (7 Slides Max)
1. **Title & Hook**: Memorable project name and crisp tagline.
2. **Problem**: Real-world friction with data or metrics.
3. **Solution**: Your product in action.
4. **Market & Audience**: Who benefits immediately.
5. **Tech Stack & Architecture**: Sonnet/Haiku/Opus triage, JEV router, vector store.
6. **Business / Growth Model**: How it scales.
7. **Team**: Strengths, handles, and call-to-action.

### 3. Judge Q&A Preparation:
Prepare ready-made answers for:
- "How do you handle hallucination or errors?" $\rightarrow$ JEV deterministic router + Opus Advisor escalation.
- "What happens when traffic spikes?" $\rightarrow$ Prompt caching + Haiku parallel subagents.
- "Is this secure?" $\rightarrow$ AgentShield baseline + secret isolation.
